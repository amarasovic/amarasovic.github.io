      <div style="font-size: var(--text-lg); line-height: 1.8; color: var(--text-secondary);">
        <p>I'm an assistant professor in the <a href="https://www.cs.utah.edu/">Kahlert School of Computing</a> at the <a href="https://www.utah.edu/">University of Utah</a>. My research interests broadly fall into <strong>NLP</strong>, <strong>human-centered AI</strong>, and <strong>interpretability</strong>.</p>
        
        <p>Previously, I was a Young Investigator at the <a href="https://allenai.org/">Allen Institute for AI</a> (2019–2022), working with <a href="https://nasmith.github.io/">Noah A. Smith</a> and <a href="https://homes.cs.washington.edu/~yejin/">Yejin Choi</a>, and held a courtesy appointment in the <a href="https://www.cs.washington.edu/">Paul G. Allen School of Computer Science & Engineering</a> at the University of Washington.</p>
        
        <p>I earned my PhD from the <a href="https://www.cl.uni-heidelberg.de/nlpgroup/">Heidelberg University NLP Group</a>, where I was advised by <a href="https://www.cl.uni-heidelberg.de/~frank/">Anette Frank</a>. Prior to receiving my PhD in 2019, I completed B.Sc. (2013) and M.Sc. (2015) in Mathematics at the University of Zagreb.</p>
        
        <p>I grew up in <a href="https://youtu.be/Cnrjm-Le_vw">Omiš, Croatia</a>. One would think I was always appreciative of mountains living in a place like that, but it took moving to Seattle to realize that free time is best spent outdoors.</p>
